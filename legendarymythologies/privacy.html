Legendary Mythologies RPG - Privacy Policy

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital)
App: Legendary Mythologies RPG (Android package com.cpdigital.legendarymythologies)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Legendary Mythologies RPG is a free 3D action role-playing game supported by advertising. It can be played offline; an optional online co-op mode lets up to 3 players play together, and an optional button saves your progress with Google Play Games. The game does not ask you to create an account with us and does not collect personal data. The third parties that process data are Google AdMob (ads), Google Play Games (only if you use the cloud save) and, only when you play online, our game server hosted by Cloudflare.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small local save file with your progress and settings (completed levels, inventory, equipment, character level and statistics, chosen hero, graphics, controls, language and sound options). This file stays on your device and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Cloud save with Google Play Games (optional)

If you press the "Google" button and sign in with Google Play Games, the game stores a copy of the same save file in your Google account ("saved games") so you can continue on another device. This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; Google Play Games may use your Play Games profile (gamer name and identifier). We do not receive your email address or other account details. You can delete the saved game from your Google Play Games settings.

Online co-op (optional)

When you choose "Online", the game connects to our game server (Cloudflare Workers, operated by Cloudflare, Inc.) to put you in a shared room with other players. The server receives only what the game needs to play together: a room code, your chosen hero and appearance, the positions and actions of the characters and enemies. There is no chat and no free text. Room data is kept in memory only while the room is open and is not stored afterwards. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the connection, under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. We do not use this data to identify you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad in menus and on the map, and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you return to the map after a level). Ads are never shown during a fight. Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one; every such button says "Watch an ad". Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • app interactions related to ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time from the game options ("Privacy choices"). If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Synthetic voices

The voices in the game are generated by computer (text-to-speech) and do not belong to real people. No voice or audio is recorded from you.

Children

Legendary Mythologies RPG is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the ads, please contact us and we will help you address it.

Data we do not collect

The game does not ask for an account with us, does not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone or precise location, does not use analytics SDKs other than the ones included in AdMob and Google Play Games, and does not contain in-app purchases.

Security and retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Online rooms exist only in memory while they are open. Data processed by Google and Cloudflare is retained according to their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For data processed by Google, please use the controls described above and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, contact us.
