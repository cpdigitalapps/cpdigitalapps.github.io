Tachivo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Tachivo: GPS Speedometer HUD ("the app", package com.cpdigital.tachivo) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Location

The app measures speed, distance, altitude and direction with your phone's GPS, so it asks for precise location. After you start a trip, it keeps measuring in the background, with the screen off or another app open, and shows a notification until you pause the trip. Tachivo does not save your coordinates or send them to us: they are used only on your device to calculate the values on screen.

Data that stays on your device

Your settings and the totals of the current trip (distance, time, average and maximum speed) are stored on your device and never sent to us. If Android backup is active on your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under your control. The app has no account, no login and no server of ours.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner in the settings, an occasional full-screen ad after you reset a trip while parked, and an optional video that you choose to watch, always labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the app's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes the data on your device, and resetting a trip deletes its totals. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
