TITLE (max 30)
Nowerland: Alice's Adventure

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
A 3D story adventure in Alice's dream: puzzles, voices, offline and no energy

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Follow the White Rabbit down the hole and step into the strangest dream ever told.

Nowerland is a 3D adventure that follows Lewis Carroll's classic story chapter by chapter. Explore every place of the book, talk with its famous characters and solve puzzles by changing your size: tiny enough to slip through a cat-flap, tall enough to look over the treetops.

ELEVEN CHAPTERS FROM THE BOOK
The river bank and the long fall down the rabbit hole, the hall of doors with the little golden key, the pool of tears and the Caucus-race, the twins Tweedledum and Tweedledee, the White Rabbit's house, the Caterpillar on the mushroom, the Duchess's peppery kitchen and the grinning Cheshire Cat, the mad tea party, the Queen's croquet game with flamingos and hedgehogs, the Lobster Quadrille by the sea and the trial of the stolen tarts.

GROW AND SHRINK
Drink from the bottle, eat the little cake, nibble the two sides of the mushroom. Every place has paths and objects that only a tiny or a giant Alice can reach.

NEVER STUCK
Your goal is always on screen. If you need help, the hint button gives you three levels of hints, from a small clue to the exact solution.

FULLY VOICED IN 16 LANGUAGES
Every character speaks, with dialogues taken from the book.

THE BOOK OF PLAYING CARDS
52 playing cards are hidden in the chapters. Find all the cards of a chapter to open a page of the Book, with John Tenniel's original illustrations and a famous line from the story.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
- Story mode with automatic saving
- Time Trial: finish each chapter as fast as you can
- Watch Hunt: find the golden pocket watches hidden in each place
- Stroll: walk freely without puzzles and take photos

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
- Simple touch controls: move with the joystick or just tap where you want to go
- Graphics options for every phone
- Plays offline: no internet needed
- No energy, no timers, no lives: play as long as you want

Free to play with ads. Ads appear only in the main menu and between some chapters, never while you play. You can also watch an optional video ad to get a hint right away.

The character voices are synthetic voices generated by computer.

Nowerland is an independent game inspired by Lewis Carroll's public domain books. It is not affiliated with any film or other adaptation.

RELEASE NOTES (max 500)
