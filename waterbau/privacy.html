Water Bau - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Water Bau: Harpoon Fishing ("the game", package com.cpdigital.waterbau) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the game handles and why.

Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, harpoons, friends, album, missions and settings) is stored on your device. It is never sent to us. If Android backup is active on your device, Android may include it in your own device backup. The save code you can copy from the game is created on your device and goes only where you paste it.

The game has no account of ours and no server of ours. Signing in with Google is optional and only used for saved games.

Optional Google saved games

On Android you can choose to keep a copy of your game in your Google account, so that you can continue on another phone or after reinstalling the game. This feature is off until you choose "Connect to Google" and sign in with Google Play Games.

When it is on, the game uses Google Play Games Services ("Saved Games"), a service provided by Google, to store your game progress (coins, harpoons, friends, album, missions and settings). The save does not contain your name, email address or other personal data. To sign you in, Google Play Games uses your Google account and your Play Games player ID; this happens between your phone and Google, under Google's rules.

The saves are sent directly from your phone to Google's servers over encrypted connections and are kept by Google in your account. We do not receive them and cannot see them. You can delete the data that Google Play Games keeps for this game from your Google account (Google Play Games settings or myaccount.google.com). How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: banners in the menus, full-screen ads in natural breaks between games, and optional videos on buttons labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ad is loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button on the Info page of the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all local data. Data processed by Google for advertising or saved games can be managed through your Google account and the Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
