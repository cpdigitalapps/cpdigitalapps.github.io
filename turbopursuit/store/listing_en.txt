TITLE:
Turbo Pursuit: Highway Racer

SHORT:
Highway racing in heavy traffic with police chases, cars and bikes. Play offline

FULL:
Turbo Pursuit is a 3D highway racing game in real traffic. Weave between cars, vans and trucks at full speed, overtake as close as you dare and try to shake off the police when you push your luck.

POLICE CHASES
Drive too fast or crash too often and the sirens start. Up to 5 wanted stars: more patrol cars, roadblocks across the lanes, spike strips and a helicopter with a searchlight. Escape them or get busted.

21 VEHICLES: CARS AND MOTORBIKES
Sports cars, classic coupes, everyday cars and 6 motorbikes, from a chopper to a race SuperGP that leans into every bend. Every bike has its own riding position.

GARAGE AND TUNING
Paint and finish (gloss, metallic, matte, pearl, chrome), brake calipers, bumpers, side skirts, spoilers, wide body kit, rims from 16 to 20 inches and tires. Upgrade engine, turbo, gearbox, suspension, brakes, grip and nitro: every upgrade changes how the car drives.

16 GAME MODES
Free ride, time attack, overtakes, always fast, distance, duel, two way traffic, clean run, rush hour, race against 5 rivals, elimination, police chase, drag race, top speed, circuit race and hot lap. Plus a career with 36 events and a daily challenge with double rewards.

CIRCUITS, LANDSCAPES AND WEATHER
6 circuits, 6 landscapes (countryside, autumn, desert, mountains, forest, coast) and 10 kinds of weather: sunny morning, sunset, night with street lights, rain, thunderstorm, fog. Long curves, climbs and descents.

YOUR CAMERA, YOUR WAY
4 views (behind, far, hood, bumper) and 6 settings for each: field of view, height, distance, tilt, return speed and dynamic motion. Drag your finger to look around the car.

CONTROLS FOR EVERYONE
Buttons, joystick or tilt steering. Easy driving with assists or realistic physics with suspension, tire grip and 6 gear engine.

Plays offline, free with ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice. 16 languages.
