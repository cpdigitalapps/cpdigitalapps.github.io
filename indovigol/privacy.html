Indovigol - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Indovigol: Score Predictor ("the app", package com.cpdigital.indovigol) is published by CpDigital ("we"). It is a free game where you guess the scores of Serie A football matches and earn points. There is no betting, no real money and no prizes. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Match data

Fixtures and results are downloaded from our server, which gets them from football-data.org and keeps them in a temporary cache. The app sends no personal data with this request. Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the response, according to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Our server keeps no logs of who downloads the data.

Data that stays on your device

Your predictions, your points and the downloaded matches are stored only on your device. If Android backup is active on your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under your control. The app has no account and no login, and your predictions are never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner above the tabs, an occasional full-screen ad when you open the Points tab, and an optional video that you choose to watch to remove ads for one hour, always labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with "Privacy choices" in the app's menu.

The "app tip"

The tip shown next to each match is calculated on your device from past results only. It is a trend, not a certainty, and it is not betting advice.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, location or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Credits

Football data provided by football-data.org. Team names are used only to identify the matches; the app is not affiliated with Serie A, the clubs or football-data.org, and shows no club logos.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all the data on your device. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
