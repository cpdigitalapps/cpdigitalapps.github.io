Rusty Hills - Privacy Policy

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital)
App: Rusty Hills: Offroad Hill Race (Android package com.cpdigital.rustyhills)
Last updated: 24 October 2026

Summary

Rusty Hills is a free, offline game supported by advertising. The game itself does not collect, store or send any personal data. Your progress (coins, vehicles, upgrades, records, settings) is saved only on your device. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps a small local save file with your coins, unlocked vehicles and stages, upgrade levels, best distances and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language). This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows banner ads in the garage and on the results screen, and full-screen ads between runs. Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one (for example "Continue (watch an ad)"). Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • app interactions related to ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices inside the game. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Children

Rusty Hills is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the ads, please contact us and we will help you address it.

Data we do not collect

The game does not ask for an account, does not read your contacts, photos, files or precise location, does not use analytics SDKs other than the ones included in AdMob, and does not contain in-app purchases.

Security and retention

Since we do not receive any personal data, we do not store any on servers. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For data processed by Google, please use the controls described above and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, contact us.
