Kalvoreth: Zombie Revenge - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Kalvoreth: Zombie Revenge (package name com.cpdigital.kalvoreth, "the game"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

1. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (completed missions, coins, weapons, equipment) and your settings (controls, graphics, sound, language) only on your device. We do not have an account system and we do not store this data on our servers. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Online co-op

Online play is optional. When you use it, your device connects to our game server (hosted on Cloudflare) and your IP address is necessarily processed by the connection. The server forwards to the other players in your room: the player name you choose, your character's appearance, your campaign level and live game data (position, actions, quick messages chosen from a fixed list). This data is kept only in memory while the room is active and is not saved by us. There is no free-text chat. Please do not use your real name as your player name. Cloudflare's handling of data is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Children

The game is a shooter with violence against zombies and monsters and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your email, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money.

7. Synthetic voices

The character voices in the game are computer-generated. They are not recordings of real people and are created in advance; the game does not process your voice.

8. Security and retention

Game data stays on your device. Online room data disappears when the room closes. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also contact us and we will help you.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
