TITLE:
Mythgrave: Horde Survivors

SHORT:
Survive endless hordes of Greek myth monsters, offline action roguelite in 3D

FULL:
Mythgrave is a 3D horde survival game set in the world of Greek myths. Move your hero with one finger: your weapons fire on their own. Survive waves of skeletons, wolves, spiders, living statues and giants, collect gems, level up and pick a gift of the gods every time. Each run is different, each run makes you stronger.

EIGHT HEROES OF LEGEND
Hoplite, Huntress, Magus, Archer, Druid, Lightkeeper, Gravecaller and Berserker: every hero starts with a different weapon and bonus. Unlock them with the souls you earn and find your favourite style.

SIXTEEN WEAPONS, SIXTEEN EVOLUTIONS
Golden Spear, Zeus Lightning, Hermes Blades, Bow of Apollo, Trident of Poseidon, Gaze of Medusa, Scythe of Cronus, Discus of Achilles, Hammer of Hephaestus, Owl of Athena, Torch of Prometheus and more. Bring a weapon to its top level with the right gift and it evolves into a far stronger form.

REAL MONSTERS, REAL BOSSES
Detailed 3D creatures with true animations: risen skeletons, wolves, giant spiders, hydra spawn, marble guardians, bronze automatons, trolls and shades of the underworld. Every few minutes a champion arrives, and great bosses wait for you: the Cyclops, the Bronze Colossus and the Hydra of Lerna.

FIVE LANDS, FIVE MODES
Fight in the Ruined Agora, the woods of Arcadia, the sands of the Pharaohs, the swamps of Lerna and the depths of Tartarus. Play Classic, Endless, Boss Rush, Hardcore or the Daily Challenge, and raise the Curse level for more souls.

GROW STRONGER BETWEEN RUNS
Spend souls in the Temple of the Gods for permanent blessings, buy armours in the Armoury and see them on your hero, upgrade your weapons at the Forge of the Gods. 28 achievements and a full bestiary to complete.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
Play offline, no internet needed. No energy, no lives, no waiting. Graphics, frame rate and effects options for every phone. Available in 16 languages.

Mythgrave is free and shows ads. Videos with rewards are always your choice.
