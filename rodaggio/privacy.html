Rodaggio - Privacy Policy

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital)
App: Rodaggio: Quiz Patente (Android package com.cpdigital.rodaggio)
Last updated: 26 October 2026

Summary

Rodaggio is a free, offline app to prepare the Italian driving licence theory exam, supported by advertising. The app itself does not collect, store or send any personal data and does not require an account. Your study progress is saved only on your device. The only third party that processes data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads.

Rodaggio is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Italian Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport or the Motorizzazione Civile. The quiz questions are the official ones published by the Ministry on the Portale dell'Automobilista.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps small local files with the licence you chose, your answers and mistakes, your exam simulations and results, an unfinished quiz (so you can resume it), bookmarks, and your settings (language, text size, theme, voice, extra time, vibration). These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the app or using Settings > Reset progress deletes them.

Read aloud (text-to-speech)

If you use the speaker button or the "read questions aloud" option, the question text is read by the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google Speech Services). Rodaggio does not send the text anywhere itself; the speech engine works according to its own provider's policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads on the menus, the topic list and the result screen, and occasional full-screen ads only after you have seen the result of a quiz. Ads are never shown during a quiz or an exam simulation. Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one ("30 minutes without ads (watch an ad)"). Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • app interactions related to ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices inside the app. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Children

Rodaggio is intended for people preparing a driving licence (from age 14 for the AM licence) and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the ads, please contact us and we will help you address it.

Data we do not collect

The app does not ask for an account, does not read your contacts, photos, files or precise location, does not use the microphone or camera, does not use analytics SDKs other than the ones included in AdMob, and does not contain in-app purchases.

Security and retention

Since we do not receive any personal data, we do not store any on servers. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For data processed by Google, please use the controls described above and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, contact us.
