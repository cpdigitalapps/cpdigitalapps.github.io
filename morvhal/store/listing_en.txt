Morvhal: Dark Action RPG

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Dark fantasy hack and slash RPG, 5 heroes, epic loot and bosses, play offline

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Ash falls on Morvhal. Vaskera, the Faceless Queen, has risen from the Abyss beneath the old cathedral, and the five Seals of Mourning that held her are broken. You were hanged as a traitor in the Valley of Crows, but the rope did not kill you. Something in the ash wants you alive.

Morvhal is a dark fantasy action RPG in 3D with a full story, seen from above in the classic diagonal view. Fight through haunted valleys, drowned crypts, a broken cathedral, a desert of salt and the Threshold of the Abyss itself.

FIVE DARK HEROES
- Ironbreaker: a huge axe and a rage that grows with every wound.
- Spectre Huntress: bow, traps and quick feet, the dead never get close.
- Bonewarden: raises an army of skeletons and curses the living.
- Stormwitch: fire, frost and lightning from her staff.
- Veiled Blade: shadow steps, poison and a dance of knives.
Every hero has their own skills, attributes to spend and a second school of power to discover.

HACK AND SLASH COMBAT
Fast combos, dodges and perfect parries, skills with real impact, screen-filling spells and huge bosses: the Headless Rider, the Bog Mother, the Stone Bishop, the Salt Queen, the Ash Dragon and many more.

LOOT WORTH KEEPING
Five rarities, random properties and legendary items with unique powers. Extract the echo of a legendary item at the blacksmith and imprint its power on your favourite gear.

A WORLD TO EXPLORE
- 5 acts, 11 places and 3 cities with merchants, blacksmiths and people to talk to.
- Short crypts and dungeons full of traps, sarcophagi and guardians.
- The Pits of the Abyss: 100 timed levels to push your hero to the limit.
- Day and night, fog, storms and dynamic music that changes when the fight begins.

MANY WAYS TO PLAY
Horde, Boss Rush, Daily Challenge, Endless Crypts, Bounties, Time Trial and Fragile Soul with only one life. Add curses for bigger rewards.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
- Plays fully offline, no internet needed.
- Touch controls you can move and resize, plus keyboard, mouse and game controller.
- Many graphics options from Minimal to Ultra, 30 to 90 FPS, to run well on any phone.
- 16 languages, with the story fully voiced in every language.

Note: the voices in the game are generated by computer (synthetic speech).

Morvhal is free to play with optional ads. No energy, no timers: just you, your blade and the ash.
