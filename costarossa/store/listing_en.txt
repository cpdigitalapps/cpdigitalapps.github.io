TITLE:
Costarossa: Retro Racing 80s

SHORT:
Pixel art arcade racing on the coast, 30 tracks, light, offline and free

FULL:
Drive into the sunset in Costarossa, a pixel art arcade racer straight from the 80s and 90s!

Floor it along coastal roads, through palm trees, city streets, green hills, deserts and snowy mountains. The scenery changes during the lap, the road climbs, dips and twists, and seven rivals fight for every position.

30 TRACKS
Every track has its own curves, hills and landscapes, with day, sunset and dawn skies. Beat your best time on each one.

5 GAME MODES
- Race: eight cars, prize money by position
- Time attack: alone against the clock for gold, silver or bronze
- Elimination: every 25 seconds the last car is out
- Checkpoint: reach the gates before time runs out
- Duel: one against one, against a rival as fast as you

10 CARS AND A REAL GARAGE
From the little hatchback to the supercar, every car has its own top speed, acceleration, handling and nitro. Win money in races, buy new cars, upgrade engine, turbo, tyres and nitro tank, and paint them in 15 colours.

RETRO STYLE, YOUR WAY
Choose the look you like: crisp pixels, smooth HD, 80s TV, 8-bit, green handheld, vivid colours or black and white. Synthwave music and arcade sounds.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
- Light and offline: play anywhere, no internet needed
- Steer with buttons or by tilting the phone
- Easy, normal or hard difficulty, 1, 3 or 5 laps
- 30 or 60 frames per second
- 16 languages
- No ads while you race

Costarossa is free and contains ads only in the menus and between races. Watching a video is always your choice and doubles the prize of the race.

RELEASE NOTES:
First release: 30 tracks, 5 game modes, 10 cars with upgrades, 7 graphics styles and 16 languages.
