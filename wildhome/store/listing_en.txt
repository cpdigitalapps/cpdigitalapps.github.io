TITLE:
Wildhome: Frontier Farm 3D

SHORT:
Build your pioneer farm in 3D: crops, animals, mines and a valley to explore

FULL:
Wildhome is a cozy 3D farming game on the wild frontier. Arrive in a quiet valley with a tent and an axe, clear the land, plant your first fields and turn the wilderness into a lively pioneer village.

FARM AND HARVEST
Chop trees, split rocks, pick berries and plow the soil. Grow wheat, corn, pumpkins, carrots, sunflowers and roses. Your pioneer walks there by herself: just tap, and she follows a queue of jobs.

ANIMALS AND BUILDINGS
Raise chickens, ducks, llamas, cows, sheep and horses. Build a log cabin, a windmill, a bakery, a loom, a well and a barn, and craft flour, bread and cloth.

MINES AND GEMS
Open coal, iron and gold mines, then ruby, emerald and diamond mines. Hire helpers who work the mines for you.

A LIVING VALLEY
Day and night, seasons, rain, snow and fog banks. Light the night with lamp posts, decorate with flowers, paths and statues, meet deer, foxes and rabbits, and fill orders from your neighbours.

MORE TO DO
- A story in chapters with friendly villagers
- New lands to unlock, and you can always travel back
- Daily tasks, rewards, collections and a market
- Create your pioneer: body, skin, hair and clothes
- 15-minute autopilot when you want a break (watch an ad)

FAIR AND RELAXING
No lives and no long waits. Play offline. Ads appear only in the menu and in natural breaks, never in the first minutes. Videos are always your choice, with buttons that say "Watch an ad". Country music, 16 languages.

Build your wild home today!
