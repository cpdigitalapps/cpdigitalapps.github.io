Singer Legend: Karaoke Songs - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Singer Legend: Karaoke Songs (package name com.cpdigital.singerlegend, "the app"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

In short: no account, no chat, no purchases. Your voice, your videos and your songs stay on your phone and we never receive them. In the online rooms only a stage name chosen from fixed lists, the song, the score and ready-made reactions travel, and nothing is stored. The app shows ads through Google AdMob.

1. Data stored on the device

The app saves only on your device: your recordings (audio and, if you turn it on, video), the songs you create (backing tracks and lyrics you add yourself), scores, settings and game results. We have no account system and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Microphone and camera

  • Microphone: used only while you sing or record a backing track, after Android has asked for your permission. The voice is analysed on the phone to give the score and to apply the effects. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Camera: the front camera is off by default. It is used only if you turn on video while singing, after Android has asked for your permission. Videos stay on the device.

3. Sharing

You can share a recording or a song file you created using Android's share menu. Nothing is shared automatically: you decide what to send and to whom. Please share only songs you have the right to use. When you open a song file received from someone else, the app asks for confirmation before adding it.

4. Online rooms

The rooms let you sing the same song of the catalog with other people, each on their own phone. To make them work the app connects to our room server (Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare Inc.). Only the following is sent:

  • a stage name made from fixed lists (for example "Nightingale 42"): you cannot type a name;
  • the room code, the chosen song of the catalog, the percent of your score and ready-made reactions (clap, heart, star...).

No voice, no video, no text and no chat are sent. The server keeps a room only while someone is in it and stores nothing after that. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the data and protect the service from abuse (Cloudflare Privacy Policy). Free-entry rooms can contain strangers: this is why nobody can write or say anything there.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): banners in the menus, full-screen ads at natural pauses and, only if you choose to, videos that give a reward. Ads are never shown while you sing.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect: the advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system version, app version, language), the IP address (used for approximate location), ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crashes and performance). Google processes this data as described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

In these countries the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP) before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the settings. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • No accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking tools besides the ads SDK described above.

9. Security and retention

Your recordings stay on your device. Connections to the room server and to Google are encrypted. The room server keeps no data after a room closes; data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also write to us and we will help you.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
