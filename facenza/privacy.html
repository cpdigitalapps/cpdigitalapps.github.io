Facenza: Face & Age Editor - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Facenza: Face & Age Editor (package name com.cpdigital.facenza, "the app"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

1. Your photos stay on your device

The photos you open in the app (from your gallery, from the camera or from another photo you choose for the face swap) are edited only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else, and the app works without an internet connection. Edited photos are saved only where you choose (the Pictures/Facenza folder of your gallery) or shared only with the app you pick.

2. Face detection on the device

To apply the face effects (retouch, makeup, shape, age, hairstyles, face swap, hair and background) the app finds the face, the hair and the background in the photo with the on-device library MediaPipe (Google, Apache 2.0 license), which is included in the app. The face points and masks are calculated in the device memory while you edit and are deleted when you close the photo: they are not saved, not sent anywhere and not used to identify anyone. The app does not do face recognition and does not compare faces with any database.

3. Example people and labeling

The example people included in the app (and their hairstyles and faces) are invented: they were created with artificial intelligence and do not show real people. Photos saved with face effects contain a machine-readable note in their metadata (XMP, IPTC "digital source type") that says they were edited; photos with a face swap also show a small visible "Face edited" label. Please use the face swap only for fun and only with people who agree.

4. Data stored on your device

The app saves only your settings (language, unlocked special effects time, notices already seen) on your device. There is no account system. Uninstalling the app deletes these settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): a banner on the home and result screens, an occasional full-screen ad after saving a photo, and a video ad only when you choose to watch one to unlock the special effects. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

Your photos and the face data described above are never given to the advertising SDK. Google acts as an independent controller for advertising data. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

7. Children

The app is a photo editor for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not upload, store or look at your photos.
  • We do not keep face data and we do not do face recognition.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or precise location.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money and no subscriptions.

9. Security and retention

App data and photos stay on your device. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also contact us and we will help you.

11. Reports

To report a problem or a misuse of an effect, use Settings > Report a problem in the app or write to the address below.

12. Changes
