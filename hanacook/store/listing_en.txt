TITLE (max 30)
Hanacook: Cooking Games

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Chop, stir, fry and serve 48 recipes from 8 world kitchens. Free and offline

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Put on your apron and cook with your fingers! In Hanacook you follow every recipe step by step together with Hana, a cheerful young chef, and Miso, her kitten helper.

COOK WITH REAL GESTURES
Slide down to chop, draw circles to stir, tap at the right moment to crack eggs, hold to pour, flick up to flip the pancake, slide up and down to knead and grate, peel the vegetables and decorate the dish with a thin line of sauce. Every step has its own little game and a score from 0 to 100.

48 RECIPES FROM 8 KITCHENS
Italy: omelette, pizza, spaghetti, minestrone and more.
Japan: onigiri, ramen, sushi rolls, miso soup, okonomiyaki.
Mexico: tacos, guacamole, quesadilla, burrito, nachos.
France: crêpes, quiche, ratatouille, onion soup, crème brûlée.
India: chicken curry, naan, dal, samosa, lassi, biryani.
USA: hamburger, pancakes, hot dog, mac and cheese, brownies.
China: fried rice, dumplings, noodles, spring rolls.
Sweets: strawberry cake, cookies, pudding, ice cream, donuts, tiramisu.

MEDALS AND STARS
Finish a recipe to earn a bronze, silver or gold medal. Medals give stars, and stars open the next kitchens of the world. Nothing to buy: every recipe is unlocked by playing.

A HAND THAT SHOWS YOU HOW
Every new step starts with a little hand that shows the gesture, so you always know what to do, even the first time.

DELICIOUS DRAWINGS AND HAPPY MUSIC
Soft, appetizing drawings of every ingredient: tomatoes with their seeds, golden omelettes, melting mozzarella. The food browns in the pan and burns if you wait too long! Cheerful music, real kitchen sounds and lots of sparkles, splashes and confetti.

DISH ALBUM
Every dish you cook goes into your album with its medal. Can you fill all eight pages with gold?

AND ALSO
No energy, no lives, no waiting: play as much as you like, even without internet. 16 languages. Light and quick to start.

Hanacook is free and contains ads. Watching a video is always your choice.
