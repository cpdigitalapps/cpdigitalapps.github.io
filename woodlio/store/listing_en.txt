TITLE:
Woodlio: Wood Block Puzzle

SHORT:
Relaxing wood block puzzle: clear leaves and decorate your cozy home, offline

FULL:
Woodlio is a calm and cozy wood block puzzle. Drag the wooden pieces onto the board, fill a row or a column to clear it, and collect every leaf to win the level. Each win gives you stars, and with the stars you decorate your own home, room after room.

500 HANDMADE-FEEL LEVELS
Short, satisfying levels that grow slowly in challenge. New wood colors and boards arrive every 50 levels, so the game always feels fresh. A friendly robot tested every level: no impossible puzzles.

DECORATE 10 ROOMS
Start with a seaside living room and move on to a mountain chalet, a city loft, a lake house, a snowy lodge, a starry attic and an island bungalow. Each room has 12 objects: floor, wallpaper, curtains, sofa, lamp, plants, bookshelf, clock and a sleepy kitten in its bed. Tap any object to change its style, for free.

MORE WAYS TO PLAY
- Endless: play as long as there is space and beat your record.
- Time Rush: score as much as you can in 90 seconds.
- Daily Challenge: a new special level every day.
- Mini games: Catch the Leaves and Wood Memory.
- Daily gift: free stars every day.

MADE TO RELAX
- No lives, no timers in levels, no pressure.
- Plays fully offline.
- Light on your phone and battery.
- 5 relaxing music tracks composed for the game: pick your favorite or turn the music off in Options.
- Available in 16 languages.

FAIR ADS
Woodlio is free and shows a few ads: a banner only on the home screen, and short breaks between levels (never in your first games and never while you play). Videos are always your choice, with a clear "Watch an ad" button, and give real rewards like extra stars or new pieces.

Relax, think, clear the board and build the home of your dreams with Woodlio!
