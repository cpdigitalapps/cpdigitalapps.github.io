Novantella - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Novantella: Statistiche 6/90 ("the app", package com.cpdigital.novantella) is published by CpDigital ("we"). It is a free tool that shows statistics of past 6/90 lottery draws, generates random combinations and checks a combination against past draws. The app does not sell tickets, does not accept bets and does not handle real money. No combination has a higher chance of winning: draws are random. The app is intended for adults (18+).

Draw data

The archive of past draws is included in the app. New draws are downloaded from our server, which reads the public results pages and keeps them in a temporary cache. The app sends no personal data with this request, only the date of the last draw it already has. Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the response, according to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Our server keeps no logs of who downloads the data.

Data that stays on your device

Your settings, the combinations you check and the downloaded draws are stored only on your device. If Android backup is active on your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under your control. The app has no account and no login.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen and an occasional full-screen ad when you change section. Gambling and betting ads are blocked. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with "Privacy choices" in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is meant for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, location or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Not affiliated

The app is not affiliated with Sisal, ADM or any lottery operator. Results are shown for information only: always check them on the official sources. If gambling is becoming a problem for you, in Italy you can call the free number 800 558822 (Telefono Verde Nazionale per le problematiche legate al Gioco d'Azzardo).

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all the data on your device. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
