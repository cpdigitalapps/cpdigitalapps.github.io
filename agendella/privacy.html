Agendella - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Agendella: Beauty Planner ("the app", package com.cpdigital.agendella, for Android and Windows) is published by CpDigital ("we"). It is an appointment book for hairdressers and beauticians. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Data that stays on your device

The price list, the clients you enter (name, phone, e-mail, notes), the appointments and the settings are stored on your device. We have no server and we never receive this data.

You, as the salon, decide which client data to enter and you are responsible for it towards your clients (for example under the GDPR). Enter only what you need for the appointments.

Saving on Google Drive (optional)

If you tap "Sign in with Google" in the Settings, the app asks your permission for one Google Drive scope only: drive.appdata. With it the app saves one file (agendella_data.json) with your salon data in the hidden application data folder of your own Google Drive, so that the PC and the phone signed in with the same account show the same agenda. The app cannot see, read or change any other file of your Google Drive. The app also reads the e-mail address of your Google account, only to show you which account is connected.

The data travels directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection and is never sent to us. You can stop at any time with "Sign out" in the Settings, and remove the app's access and its hidden data from your Google account (Google Drive, Settings, Manage apps, Agendella, Delete hidden app data; or myaccount.google.com, Security, Third-party apps).

Agendella's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide the saving and syncing feature, it is not used for advertising, it is not sold and it is not transferred to anyone.

Advertising (Google AdMob, Android only)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner kept away from the agenda and, only if you choose it, a video ad that is always labelled ("Watch an ad"). The Windows app has no ads. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

Your salon and client data are never shared with the advertising service. Advertising data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the Settings.

Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

Records deleted in the app are deleted from the device and, at the next sync, from the copy on your Google Drive. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on that device. The copy on Google Drive can be deleted as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
