TITLE:
Deadhaul: Zombie Extraction

SHORT:
Loot the zombie city, fill your backpack and escape alive. Offline, no lives.

FULL:
Enter the quarantine zone. Grab everything you can. Get out alive.

Deadhaul is a zombie extraction shooter seen from above. Every raid is a new city map full of crates, abandoned cars, ruins and the walking dead. Open crates, fit your loot into a grid backpack, then run to the green flare and hold the extraction point while the helicopter comes. If you die, you lose everything you carried. Do you loot one more crate, or leave now?

WHY YOU WILL LOVE IT
- Real extraction gameplay: risk, loot, escape. Every decision counts.
- Your survivor aims and shoots by himself: you focus on moving, looting and escaping.
- 5 zones: Old Town, Suburbs, Harbor, Hospital and Army Base, each with its own look, dangers and loot.
- 50 levels with different objectives: escape with enough loot, kill a number of zombies, recover the red case, escape in time, take down the giant zombie that hunts you across the map.
- Up to 3 stars per level, coin rewards and reward chests.
- 3 daily missions with extra prizes.
- Grid backpack: big items take more space. Drop junk, keep the gold.
- 4 real weapons: pistol, SMG, pump shotgun and assault rifle.
- Upgrade your safehouse: workbench, infirmary, training room, radio room and armory.
- Realistic zombies, runners and huge brutes, dynamic music that changes when the dead are close.
- Zoom the camera in or out with two fingers.

FAIR AND FREE
- No energy, no lives, no waiting.
- Everything is earned by playing: no pay walls.
- Ads only in menus and between raids. Rewarded videos are always your choice (double loot, recover half the backpack).
- Plays offline.
- 16 languages.

Deadhaul is a violent game with zombies. Not for children.
