Talkastra: AAC Speech Symbols - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Talkastra: AAC Speech Symbols (package name com.cpdigital.talkastra, "the app"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information. The app is used by children together with their parents, teachers and speech therapists. We wrote this page for parents and guardians in simple words.

In short: no account, no chat, no purchases. The words, the photos and the voices you add stay on the device and we never receive them. The child never sees ads while communicating: one banner suitable for children is shown only in the adult area, through Google AdMob, without personalized ads and without the advertising ID.

1. Data stored on the device

The app saves only on the device: the boards and the words changed or added by the adult, the photos chosen or taken by the adult, the voices recorded by the adult, the visual schedule, the words written with the keyboard (to propose them again) and the settings. We have no account system and we do not receive or store this data on any server. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The backup file is created only when the adult asks for it, and is saved where the adult chooses (for example the Downloads folder or a cloud service of their own). It contains the boards, the photos and the voices: keep it safely. We have no access to it.

2. Camera, photos and microphone

  • Photos: when the adult adds a picture to a word, Android's photo picker lets them choose a single photo. The app does not have access to the other photos of the device.
  • Camera: when the adult chooses "Take a photo", the camera app of the device opens. The app does not use the camera in any other way.
  • Microphone: used only while the adult records a voice for a word, after Android has asked for permission. The recording stays on the device and is never sent anywhere.

3. The voice of the app

Words and sentences are spoken by the text-to-speech engine installed on the device. What the child says with the app is not recorded and is not sent to us. If the adult has chosen a text-to-speech engine that works online, that engine is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob), only in the adult area

The app is free. It shows one banner, only in the adult area (which is closed by a question for adults or a code), provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), a Google Play self-certified ads SDK for apps for children. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA, "tag for child-directed treatment") and limited to content rated G (suitable for all audiences). This means:

  • no personalized ads and no remarketing;
  • the advertising ID is not used: the app does not have the advertising ID permission;
  • no full-screen ads and no videos.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited technical information: device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language), the IP address (used for approximate location, for example the country), ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data), and an app-specific identifier (App set ID). Google processes this data as described here: AdMob and child-directed apps, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Ad requests are sent as "under the age of consent" and only non-personalized ads are shown. If Google's consent tools require it, a "Privacy choices" button is available in the settings, inside the adult area.

6. Children

The app complies with the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the app never asks for a name, age, email, phone number or location, and it has no chat and no social features. Photos, voices and names added by the adult stay on the device. Links that leave the app (this privacy page) are inside the adult area.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • No accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking tools besides the ads SDK described above.

8. Not a medical device

Talkastra helps to communicate with pictures. It is not a medical device and does not replace the advice of professionals.

9. Security and retention

The data of the app stays on the device. Data processed by Google AdMob is sent encrypted and kept according to Google's policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also write to us and we will help you.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
