TITLE:
Trenello: Train Station Game

SHORT:
Drive the train and stop right at the platform. Light, offline, no lives.

FULL:
Hold to go, let go to brake: can you stop your train exactly at the platform?

Trenello is a colourful train driving game for everyone. Drive through meadows, seaside towns, snowy forests, deserts, lavender fields, tropical islands and high mountains. Slow down on the curves, ride through tunnels and stop as close as you can to the station sign: the more precise you are, the more coins you earn.

HOW TO PLAY
• Hold your finger on the screen to accelerate, lift it to brake.
• Stop inside the green zone to pick up the passengers.
• PERFECT stops (within one metre) pay triple coins.
• Respect the speed limits on curves and watch the level crossings.

LOTS TO DO
• Endless levels in 10 different landscapes
• 4 game modes: Play, Time Trial, Endless and Daily Challenge
• 8 trains to unlock: modern, steam, bullet and golden trains
• Upgrades: engine, brakes, tickets and precision
• Weather and time of day change: sun, clouds, rain, snow, sunset and night
• 6 camera views, including the driver's cab with real gauges and wipers
• Passengers who walk to the doors and board your train
• Cheerful music and train sounds
• 16 languages

PLAY ANYWHERE
Trenello works offline and has no lives or waiting timers. Play a level in a minute or ride for as long as you like.

The game is free and contains ads. Watching a video is always your choice and gives you double coins.
