Poker Legend: 3D Texas Holdem - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Poker Legend: 3D Texas Holdem (package name com.cpdigital.pokerlegend, "the game"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

The game is for adults (18+) and is for entertainment only. It is played offline against computer-controlled opponents. It does not offer real-money gambling or any chance to win real money or prizes. Chips are virtual, have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or redeemed.

1. Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, graphics, colours, view, sounds), your chips, your character, your statistics and medals, the rooms you unlocked and the daily gift are saved only on your device. We have no server and we do not receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If Android backup is active on your device, Android may include this game data in your own Google backup.

2. No account, no chat

There is no registration: no email, no password, no phone number, no social login, no online play and no chat. The opponents' voices are synthetic voices included in the game: the game never uses your microphone.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): a banner in the menus, sometimes a full-screen ad at the end of a table or tournament, and videos that you choose to watch to get chips. Ads are never shown during a hand. Ads of real-money gambling are blocked. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time in the game: Options > Game > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Minors

The game is only for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. The game asks your age the first time you open it and closes access to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or location.
  • There are no purchases with real money, no subscriptions, and chips can never be bought or turned into money or prizes.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. The game keeps no personal data on our side; requests about advertising data are handled by Google, and we can help you.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
