TITLE:
Zombrake: Zombie Survival 3D

SHORT:
Survive 60 seconds against zombie hordes. Offline, no energy, auto-shooting hero

FULL:
Survive the zombie apocalypse, 60 seconds at a time!

Zombrake is a fast 3D action game: move with one finger, your hero aims and shoots by himself. Survive the horde for 60 seconds, collect gems, rank up and choose a new power each time. Every level is short, intense and different.

600 LEVELS IN 30 ZONES
From the suburbs of Port Ashen to the hospital, the subway, the harbor, the mall, the military base and the city on fire. Then cross the sea and head north: old town, park, school, stadium, cemetery, prison, dam, airport, mines and ground zero. Every zone has its own map, its own zombies and its own boss.

30 BOSSES
The Butcher, the Colossus, Patient Zero, the Howler, the Spore Mother, the Abyss and many more. Each boss has special attacks: charges, ground slams, acid, spores and reinforcements.

15 KINDS OF ZOMBIES
Runners, crawlers, infected dogs, exploders, spitters, armored soldiers, screamers that make the horde faster, shadows you see only up close, leapers, healers and giant brutes.

BUILD YOUR HERO
- 7 weapons to unlock and upgrade: assault rifle, twin pistols, pump shotgun, submachine gun, marksman rifle, rotary machine gun and flamethrower.
- 3 heroes with their own skills: Rex the soldier, Marta the medic, Nathan the heavy.
- Gear with 4 rarities: helmets, vests, boots and gloves.
- 18 powers to choose during the levels and 10 secret combos: chain lightning, spinning orbs, energy shield, mines, fire and frost rounds, cluster grenades and more.
- The Armory and the Shelter: permanent upgrades, a drone that flies with you and a storage that produces coins.

ALWAYS SOMETHING TO DO
- 4 modes: Campaign, Endless, Brute Assault and Daily Challenge.
- Daily and weekly missions, a 7-day gift you never lose, 3 stars for every level.
- Medals, statistics and a story in 31 chapters.
- Seasonal events with decorations and bonus coins.

FAIR AND OFFLINE
- Plays offline, no internet needed.
- No energy, no lives to wait for.
- No real-money purchases: you earn everything by playing.
- Ads only in the menus and between levels, never while you play. Optional videos always say "Watch an ad".

GRAPHICS FOR EVERY PHONE
Low, Medium, High and Ultra presets, 30 to 120 FPS, 3D resolution, shadows, effects, zoom in and out. 16 languages.

Download Zombrake and see how long you can survive!
