Ciribella: Doll House Dress Up - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Ciribella: Doll House Dress Up (package name com.cpdigital.ciribella, "the game"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information. The game is made for children. We wrote this page for parents and guardians in simple words.

In short: the game does not ask for any personal information, has no chat, no accounts and no purchases. Everything the child creates stays on the device. The game shows ads suitable for children through Google AdMob, configured for children: no personalized ads, no advertising ID.

1. Data stored on the device

The game saves the characters, clothes, the decorated house, the photos taken in the photo studio, coins, missions and settings (language, sound, music) only on the device. The photos are pictures of the game characters, made inside the game: the game does not use the camera and does not access the photos on the device. We have no account system and we do not receive or store this data on any server. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob) for children

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited), a Google Play self-certified ads SDK for apps for children. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (COPPA, "tag for child-directed treatment") and limited to content rated G (suitable for all audiences). This means:

  • no personalized ads and no remarketing;
  • the advertising ID is not used: the game does not have the advertising ID permission;
  • ads are full-screen videos or pictures that can be closed, shown only in natural pauses (for example after a fashion show) or when the child chooses a button that clearly says "Watch an ad" to get coins or unlock an item. These buttons are always optional.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process limited technical information: device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language), the IP address (used for approximate location, for example the country), ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data), and an app-specific identifier (App set ID). Google processes this data as described here: AdMob and child-directed apps, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Because the game is for children, ad requests are sent as "under the age of consent" and only non-personalized ads are shown: no consent form appears for personalized advertising. If Google's consent tools require it, a "Privacy choices" button is available in the game settings, behind a small question for grown-ups.

4. Children

The game is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: the game never asks for a name, age, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and it has no chat and no social features. Links that leave the game (this privacy page) are protected by a question for grown-ups.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • No accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or tracking tools besides the ads SDK described above.

6. Security and retention

Game data stays on the device. Data processed by Google AdMob is sent encrypted and kept according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also write to us and we will help you.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
