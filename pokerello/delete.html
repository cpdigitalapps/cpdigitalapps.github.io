Pokerello: Texas Holdem Poker - Delete your data

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital). This page explains how to delete your online guest profile of the game Pokerello: Texas Holdem Poker (com.cpdigital.pokerello) and the data linked to it.

Delete from the game (immediate)

  1. Open Pokerello.
  2. Tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete profile and data and confirm.

Your online profile is deleted from our server at once, and your offline chips, statistics and settings are deleted from your device.

Delete without the game

If you no longer have the game, write to cpdigital83@proton.me with the subject "Pokerello - delete my profile" and, if you can, your player ID (shown in the game in Settings > Information). We delete the profile within 30 days and confirm by email.

In any case, a profile that is not used for 12 months is deleted automatically.

What is deleted

  • the guest profile: player ID, secret key, nickname, face;
  • online chips, skill rating, numbers of hands and tournaments, daily gift and ads counters;
  • the reports you sent about other players.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our server after the deletion. Data processed by Google AdMob for ads is handled by Google (Google Privacy Policy).

See also the Privacy Policy.
