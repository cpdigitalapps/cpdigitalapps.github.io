Pokerello: Texas Holdem Poker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the game Pokerello: Texas Holdem Poker (package name com.cpdigital.pokerello, "the game"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

The game is for adults (18+) and is for entertainment only. It does not offer real-money gambling or any chance to win real money or prizes. Chips are virtual, have no monetary value and cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or redeemed.

1. Data stored on your device

Your settings (language, sounds, help at the table, card colours), your offline chips, your statistics and the history of your last hands are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

2. Online play: your guest profile

There is no registration: no email, no password, no phone number and no social login. The first time you open the online part, our game server creates a guest profile with:

  • a random player ID and a secret key (the key is stored on your device and, in a scrambled form that cannot be read back, on the server);
  • a nickname made of two words chosen by the game from fixed lists (you cannot type a name) and a face chosen from a gallery;
  • your online chips, your skill rating, the number of hands and tournaments you played, the daily gift and the ads watched today;
  • the time you were last seen, and the chips you were holding at a table (so that they come back to you if the server restarts).

During a game the server receives your moves, the random numbers your device sends for the sealed deck check, and the ready-made phrases and faces you send. There is no free chat: other players only see your nickname, your face, your chips and your moves. Messages are not stored.

If you report a player, we store the time of the report, the two player IDs and the table, to stop abuse. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

Your IP address is used by the server only to keep the connection open and to protect the game from floods of messages; it is not saved in our database.

The game server runs on Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.), which processes the connection on our behalf as a service provider.

3. How long we keep your profile and how to delete it

A guest profile that is not used for 12 months is deleted automatically.

You can delete your profile and all its data at any time in the game: Settings > Delete profile and data. If you no longer have the game, see Delete your data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Ads are never shown during a hand. Ads of real-money gambling are blocked. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

6. Minors

The game is only for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. The game asks your age the first time you open it. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the game, contact us with the player ID and we will delete the profile.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • There are no purchases with real money, no subscriptions, and chips can never be bought or turned into money or prizes.

8. Security

The connection between the game and our server is encrypted (TLS). The secret key of your profile is never stored in a readable form on the server.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise them, write to us with your player ID (shown in Settings > Information). Requests about advertising data are handled by Google; we can help you.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its date.
