Ruin Rush - Privacy Policy

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital) · App: Ruin Rush for Android (package name com.cpdigital.ruinrush) · Effective date: 24 October 2026

This page explains what happens to your data when you play Ruin Rush, a free game published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital) ("we", "us"). We wrote it in plain language because we want you to actually read it.

1. In short

  • The game works completely offline and has no server of its own. Your progress stays on your phone.
  • The game itself does not collect, store or send any personal data about you.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google's advertising software may collect some technical data, described in section 4.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's settings.
  • No account, no sign-in, no sale of your data.

2. What the app stores on your device

To work as a game, Ruin Rush saves a few things in its own private storage on your phone:

  • your best score and your total coins;
  • which character looks you have unlocked and which one you use;
  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, language) and your privacy choices;
  • simple counters used to limit how often ads appear (for example, how many runs since the last ad).

This information never leaves your device through the app. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data in Android settings.

3. What the app itself does not do

  • It does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, files or precise location.
  • It has no server of ours and includes no analytics or tracking tools of ours.
  • It does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone for purposes other than the advertising described below.

4. Advertising: Google AdMob

Ruin Rush is free and is supported by ads shown through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app. Ads appear only in the menus and after a run: a banner, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example, to continue after a crash).

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (not GPS);
  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers, plus device information such as model, operating system version, language, screen size and network type;
  • App interactions related to ads, such as which ads were shown, clicked or watched to the end;
  • Crash logs and diagnostics about the ad components.

This data is used for advertising and marketing (choosing and showing ads, measuring their performance, limiting how often you see them), for analytics, and for fraud prevention, security and compliance. Depending on your consent, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google processes this data under its own rules. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We, the developer, do not receive personal data from AdMob. We only see aggregated statistics such as the number of ads shown and the estimated earnings.

5. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game you will see a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can accept, refuse ("Do not consent") or manage your options purpose by purpose and partner by partner. If you refuse, the game still works and ads may still appear, but they will be limited or non-personalized.

You can review or change your choice at any time: open the game, go to Settings > Privacy choices.

6. Advertising ID

Android gives each device an advertising ID that apps may use for ads. You control it from your phone: open Android Settings > Google > Ads (on recent versions: Settings > Privacy > Ads). There you can reset the ID, turn off ad personalization or delete the advertising ID altogether. If you delete it, the app can no longer use it for personalized ads.

7. Children

Ruin Rush is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under the age of digital consent in your country). Its Google Play listing is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the ads in the game, please contact us at the address below and we will help you have it removed.

8. Data retention

We keep nothing: no data reaches us. The information described in section 2 stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data processed by Google through AdMob is kept according to Google's retention rules, described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

9. Your rights

If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws such as the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw your consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Because we hold no personal data about you, the quickest way to exercise these rights is through the controls already in your hands: the Privacy choices screen in the game, the advertising ID settings of Android (section 6), and Google's own controls at https://myadcenter.google.com. You are always welcome to email us with any question or request, and we will answer.

10. Security

The Google Mobile Ads SDK communicates with Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We keep no copies of your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the amount of data involved in this game is deliberately kept to the minimum needed to show ads.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add features to the game or when the advertising software changes. The current version is always available at this address, with the effective date shown at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the release notes of the app update.
