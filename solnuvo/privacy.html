Solnuvo - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Solnuvo: Weather & Forecast ("the app", package com.cpdigital.solnuvo) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Location

To show the weather where you are, the app asks for your approximate location (it never asks for precise location). You can refuse and search for a city instead. When the forecast is downloaded, the coordinates are rounded to two decimals (about 1 km) and sent to our forecast server, which forwards them to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway) to get the forecast. Our server keeps forecasts in a temporary cache by rounded coordinates only; it does not store who asked for them and keeps no history of your positions. Our server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address to deliver the response, according to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

City names are found with the location services built into Android (Geocoder), which on most phones are provided by Google.

Data that stays on your device

Your saved cities, your settings (units) and the last forecast are stored only on your device. If Android backup is active on your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under your control. The app has no account and no login.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the weather screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you go back from the list of places, and an optional video that you choose to watch to remove ads for one hour, always labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the app's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Weather data

Forecasts come from MET Norway and are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Forecasts are estimates and can be wrong; for weather warnings always follow your official national services.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all the data on your device; you can also remove saved cities in the app. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
