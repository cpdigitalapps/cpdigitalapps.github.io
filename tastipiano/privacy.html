Tasti Piano: Learn Piano - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how the app Tasti Piano: Learn Piano (package name com.cpdigital.tastipiano, "the app"), published by CpDigital (Claudio Digital), handles information.

1. Data stored on your device

The app saves your progress (lessons completed, stars, points, minutes of practice, days in a row) and your settings (language, note names, labels on the keys, finger numbers, daily goal) only on your device. There is no account system and we do not receive or store this data on our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Microphone, camera and MIDI keyboards

The app does not use the microphone or the camera. If you connect a MIDI keyboard, the notes you play are used only on your device to follow the lesson or the song; nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). Ads are never shown while you play or during a lesson. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID);
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

Google acts as an independent controller for this data. How Google uses it is described here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown. You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Children

The app is a music course for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • There are no in-app purchases with real money and no subscriptions.

7. Security and retention

App data stays on your device. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, requests about advertising data are handled by Google; you can also contact us and we will help you.
