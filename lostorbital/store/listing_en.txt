TITLE:
Lost Orbital: Space Ninja 3D

SHORT:
Sci-fi ninja action: katana, plasma guns and powers in a 22 mission campaign

FULL:
Lost Orbital is a fast third-person sci-fi action game. You are Ronin, a cyborg ninja with a thermal katana, plasma weapons and four special powers. A strange signal has woken the machines of the system: board silent space stations, jump across broken decks over the void and face giant guardians to find out who is sending it.

A STORY IN 22 MISSIONS
Two parts and nine chapters: orbital stations, alien laboratories, an ice moon, a red desert, the forges of Ferrum, a city floating in the clouds, an ancient temple and the station of the Echo around a dying star. Vela, the mind of your ship, guides you and has a secret of her own.

NINJA MOVES
Double jump, dash in the air and slide. Jump from platform to platform over bottomless chasms, run up the stairs to high walkways and strike from above.

KATANA, PLASMA AND POWERS
Slice enemies with the thermal katana or use plasma rifles, pistols and snipers. Every armor has four powers: shockwave, energy shield, blink, chain lightning, slam, storm, frost, gravity and more.

8 ARMORS TO BUILD
Collect alloy, crystals and cores, build new armors in the Foundry and level up armors and weapons. Equip mods of four rarities and merge copies to make them stronger.

ENEMIES AND BOSSES
Cyborgs, gunners, shielded troopers, exploding drones, mutants and huge bosses: the Warden, the Colossus, the Void Maw and the Devourer.

MORE TO PLAY
Reactor defense with endless waves and a personal leaderboard, special operations (sabotage, rescue, stealth), daily and weekly missions, a daily login gift, trophies, a creature codex, colors for your armor and a ship base to walk around.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Touch controls designed for phones, full keyboard, mouse and controller support, six difficulty levels, graphics options with HDR, better lighting and V-Sync, and 16 languages.

Free to play with ads. Optional videos are always marked "Watch an ad" and give real rewards. No energy timers, no in-app purchases.
