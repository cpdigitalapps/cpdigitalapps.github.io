Montaro 4K - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Montaro 4K: Video Editor ("the app", package com.cpdigital.montaro) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Your videos, photos and projects stay on your device

The app edits the videos, photos and music that you choose with the Android photo picker or file picker. It only reads the files you select. Your projects, the edited videos (saved in Movies/Montaro) and your settings are stored on your device and are never sent to us. The app has no account, no login and no server of ours.

Microphone

If you use the "Voice" tool, the app asks for the microphone to record your voice-over. The recording is saved only on your device, inside your project, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Automatic subtitles

Subtitles are created on your device, without internet: the speech in your videos is never sent anywhere. The first time you use a language, the app downloads its voice model (a file of about 40-80 MB) from alphacephei.com, the site of the open-source Vosk project. This download sends no personal data and no audio; like any download, the server receives your IP address.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen, an occasional full-screen ad after you save a video, and optional videos that you choose to watch to unlock some tools, always labelled "Watch ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the app's Options.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, your videos, photos, recordings or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Deleting a project removes it from your device (your original videos and photos stay in your gallery). Uninstalling the app deletes all its data on your device. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
