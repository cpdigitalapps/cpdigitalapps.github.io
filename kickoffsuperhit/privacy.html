Kickoff Super Hit - Privacy Policy

Developer: CpDigital (Claudio Digital)
App: Kickoff Super Hit: Soccer 3D (Android package com.cpdigital.kickoffsuperhit)
Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Kickoff Super Hit is a free arcade 3D football game supported by advertising. It can be played offline; an optional online mode lets you play against other people or together in the same team. The game does not ask for an account and does not collect personal data for itself. Your progress is saved on your device. The third parties that process data are Google AdMob (ads), our game server hosted by Cloudflare (only when you play online) and Google Play Games Services (only if you choose to save your progress to your Google account).

Data stored on your device

The game keeps small local files with your progress and settings: your team (name, colours, kit, player stats and training points), missions, cup and league progress, language, sound, graphics and gameplay options. These files never leave your device unless you use the optional Google save described below, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

Online mode (optional)

When you choose "Online", the game connects to our game server (Cloudflare Workers, operated by Cloudflare, Inc.) to put you in a room with other players, either by a room code or by quick matching. The server receives only what the game needs to play together: your team name and colours, the player name shown in the room, your team's player stats, and the moves of the match (positions of players and ball, button presses). There is no chat. Room data is kept in memory only while the room is open and is not stored afterwards. Please do not use your real name or other personal information as a team or player name. Like any internet service, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the connection, under Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. We do not use this data to identify you.

Saving to your Google account (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the game can store a copy of your progress in your Google account ("Saved Games"), so you can recover it on another device. This uses Google Play Games Services and Google receives your Play Games player identity and the saved progress, under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your email address or other account details. You can delete the saved data from your Google Play Games settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad only on the title screen and an occasional full-screen ad after a match has ended, never during play and never in your first matches. Rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one ("Watch an ad: +3 training points"). Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • the Android advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • app interactions related to ads (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs.

This data is processed by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can refuse, and you can change your choice at any time from Options > Privacy choices. If you refuse, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised.

Synthetic voices

The match commentary uses synthetic voices generated by computer (Supertonic 3 and Kokoro). No recordings of real people's voices are used and the game never records your voice.

Children

Kickoff Super Hit is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the ads or the online mode, please contact us and we will help you address it.

Data we do not collect

The game does not ask for an account, does not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone or precise location, does not use analytics SDKs other than the ones included in AdMob, and does not contain in-app purchases.

Security and retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Online rooms exist only in memory while they are open. Data processed by Google and Cloudflare is retained according to their policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. For data processed by Google, please use the controls described above and Google's privacy tools. For anything else, contact us.
