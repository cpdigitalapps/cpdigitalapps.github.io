TITLE: Brontak: Mech Survival Shooter
SHORT: Pilot a giant robot, blast monster hordes and rescue survivors. Works offline.
NOTES: First release of Brontak! Pilot a giant combat robot across 40 levels in 4 zones, fight 9 kinds of monsters and 5 huge bosses, rescue survivors and build your shelter. Available in 16 languages, works offline, no ads during missions.
FULL:
Take control of BRONTAK, a giant combat robot, and march through a world overrun by monsters.

Your robot fires on its own: just drag your finger to move it, dodge enemy attacks and choose the best path. Fly through blue gates to power up your cannons, avoid the red ones, and save every survivor you can find on the road.

40 LEVELS IN 4 ZONES
- Flooded city at sunset
- Scorching desert among the mesas
- Frozen city under the polar aurora
- Lands of fire with rivers of lava
Each zone has a mini-boss and a huge final boss: the Sand Worm, the Ice Colossus, the Lava Spider and the Swarm Queen.

9 KINDS OF MONSTERS
Swarmers, brutes, acid spitters, flying harpies, exploding bloaters, armored chargers, nests, leapers and serpents. Every zone mixes them in a different way.

HANGAR
- 4 robots with different armor, damage and cannons
- Upgrade power, fire rate, armor and ability
- 3 commanders with special abilities: missile storm, energy shield and overload

SHELTER
The survivors you rescue live in your shelter and keep producing credits, even when the game is closed. Expand the workshop, the dormitory and the laboratory.

FAIR AND OFFLINE
- Works offline, no account needed
- No ads during the missions
- Optional videos are always your choice
- No in-app purchases
- Graphics quality options for every phone
- 16 languages
