Ancient Woods - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Ancient Woods: Forest Village ("the game", package com.cpdigital.ancientwoods) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the game handles and why.

Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (energy, resources, buildings, quests, team and settings) is stored on your device. It is never sent to us. If Android backup is active on your device, Android may include it in your own device backup.

The game has no account of ours and no server of ours.

Optional Google saved games

If this feature is available in your version and you choose "Save to Google", your game progress is stored in your own Google Play Games account, under Google's terms. We do not receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: banners in the menus, full-screen ads in natural breaks, and optional videos on buttons labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ad is loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the game settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all local data. Data processed by Google for advertising or saved games can be managed through your Google account and the Android settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
