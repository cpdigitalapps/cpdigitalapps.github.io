Fenno - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Fenno: Super Fox Adventure ("the game", package com.cpdigital.fenno) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the game handles and why.

Data that stays on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, gems, coins, outfits, settings and statistics) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us. The "save code" in Settings is generated on your device and is only transferred if you copy and paste it yourself.

The game works completely offline. It has no account, no login and no server of ours.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows advertising through Google AdMob. There are no ads during the levels. A small banner appears in the menus, a full-screen ad may appear occasionally after a completed level, and video ads are optional and always labelled as such ("Watch an ad"). To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before any personalised ad is loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in Settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game deletes all local data. "Reset progress" in Settings deletes it too. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
