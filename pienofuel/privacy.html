PienoFuel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

PienoFuel: Distributori Vicini ("the app", package com.cpdigital.pienofuel) is published by CpDigital ("we"). This page explains what data the app handles and why.

Location

To show the fuel stations near you, the app asks for your location. You can refuse and search for an Italian town instead. Your position never leaves your phone: the app downloads the complete list of Italian stations and prices and calculates distances on the device. We do not have a server and we never receive your position.

Fuel price data

Prices and stations are downloaded directly from the open data published by the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy (MIMIT, Osservaprezzi Carburanti, licence IODL 2.0) at www.mimit.gov.it. To deliver the files, the Ministry's web server receives your IP address, as for any website visit; no other data is sent. PienoFuel is an independent app and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body. Prices are those in force at 8:00 of the previous day and the price at the pump may differ.

Data that stays on your device

The downloaded price files, your settings (fuel type, radius, litres, consumption) and the last town you searched are stored only on your device. If Android backup is active on your phone, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under your control. The app has no account and no login.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad when you come back to the app after opening the navigator (never in the first uses), and an optional video that you choose to watch to remove ads for 30 minutes, always labelled "Watch an ad". To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • the advertising ID of your device and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your interactions with ads, app performance data and crash logs.

This data is handled by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google advertising policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in the Android settings.

Your consent (Europe, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows a consent message (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are loaded. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices, and you can change them at any time with the "Privacy choices" button in the app's settings.

Navigation

When you tap a station, the app asks your phone to open a maps or navigation app of your choice with the station's position. What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, positions or any other personal data ourselves. There are no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all the data on your device. Data processed by Google for advertising can be managed through your Google account and the Android advertising settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
